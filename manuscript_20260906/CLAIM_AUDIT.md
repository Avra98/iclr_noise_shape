# Claim and proof audit

This is an ICLR-style working manuscript, not a claim of acceptance or established literature priority. The main text is nine pages. Classical results are credited; explicit constructions, derived central-law statements, and finite-time observations are distinguished.

1. **Scalar quadratic product ordering — proved directly.** Moments use absolute gain: m(p)=E|a|^p, gamma=E log|a|, H=1/m(-1). Jensen and the iid product identity establish the ordering. Negative-order contraction means inverse-distance decay; it is not ordinary perturbation contraction.
2. **Scalar central renewal law — proved for the specified returned model.** Positive local logarithmic drift plus a hard amplitude cap gives a reflected logarithmic random walk. Exponential tilt and the classical overshoot theorem give the small-ball exponent m(-alpha)=1. The density statement has a concrete sufficient assumption (bounded continuous compactly supported log-increment density) and a ladder-renewal proof. A separate exponential-log example has an exact invariant density, including its boundary atoms. This is a central application of classical renewal, not a newly discovered reflected-walk theorem.
3. **Matrix central law — proved under explicit spectral, angular-mixing and entry assumptions.** Radius and direction evolve jointly. The appendix derives a uniform interval-occupation bound, the all-start renewal extension, the killed-potential coefficient and its positivity criterion. A symmetric noncommuting reflection example verifies every assumption. The negative matrix root is not a fixed-direction Rayleigh moment.
4. **Nonlinear transfer — a conditional theorem, not a verified neural corollary.** A signed occupation-source identity and weighted kernel discrepancy suffice. Entry control, finiteness and positive coefficient are additional obligations. A Taylor expansion alone does not supply them. Fixed additive noise changes the literal zero-radius limit.
5. **Smooth nonconvex SGD return — proved.** Every sampled loss is coercive. Outer contraction plus bounded nonlinear remainder and nondegenerate Gaussian forcing gives a unique smooth attracting invariant law with all polynomial moments. The eta=2.015 instance has gamma0>0, H0>1 and a frozen inverse root alpha0≈2.01743. The root is not claimed as the noisy invariant density exponent. This return argument has substantial prior overlap with nonlinear random-recursion theory.
6. **Positive stationary occupied exponent — proved for the explicit piecewise-smooth control.** Its two-step minorization and occupied derivative magnitudes give a unique invariant law and lambda_pi=0.18176569... at eta=1, with a positive lower bound throughout eta in [0.98,1]. The loss is C1/piecewise C2. The outside-start derivative annihilation at eta=1 is retained in the proof. This is a concrete SGD realization of a classical random tent-map mechanism, not broad chaos novelty.
7. **Matrix projection counterexample — proved.** The isotropic nonquadratic regression construction has a stationary law beyond local matrix logarithmic and negative-moment references. In dimension at least three, every fixed projection is centrally unimodal. That construction is convex nonquadratic, distinct from the nonconvex scalar example. Pointwise ambient-density scaling elsewhere in the manuscript requires joint local density convergence.
8. **Neural observations — empirical and finite time.** The main MLP has negative late fixed-top scalar log gain, positive transported growth and approximately period-two two-lobe occupancy under iid batches. The initial batch-gradient law exceeds both scalar references. These are different objects and windows. The selected CNN fixed-top candidate fails confirmation. Other random-axis candidates exist and are not silently excluded by a blanket no-bimodality claim.
9. **Wider evidence — retained with its scope.** Additional architecture heatmaps preserve missing probe cells and recorded cutoffs. GPT was trained with Adam: raw and frozen-preconditioner curvature plots are auxiliary scalar diagnostics, not the full Adam-state Jacobian. Smooth/constructed-network long-run growth rates are numerical estimates; recurrence for the constructed models is proved separately. Ordinary-network drift and cutoff escapes remain visible.

## Independent review and repairs incorporated

The scalar and matrix modules were cross-reviewed independently. The final text incorporates the joint-density qualification; the zero-additive-noise diffusion condition a>nu^2/2; the distinction between infinitesimal tangent growth and finite trajectory separation; and the CNN fixed-top versus random-axis distinction. Numerical audits reconstruct displayed statistics from saved gains or trajectory summaries. No old draft PDF measurement was substituted for the audited current data.

## What remains a research question

- A verified return and central-renewal transfer theorem for ordinary neural SGD in a specified parameter, quotient, or prediction state space.
- A proved harmonic-to-bimodality transition for those neural networks.
- An analytic sign proof for the smooth examples' measured stationary tangent exponents.
- A comprehensive literature-priority argument for a novel general theorem beyond the applications and constructions here.

The manuscript makes none of these unresolved claims as established theorems.
